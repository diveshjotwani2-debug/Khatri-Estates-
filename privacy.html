Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Khatri Estates handles through this website. Khatri Estates is a real estate broker agency based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, operated by [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME].

Information you give us

The enquiry form asks for your name and phone number, and optionally the property you are interested in, a preferred site visit date and a message. The form does not send this information to us by itself. It prepares a message that you can send to us through WhatsApp or Instagram. We receive it only when you press send there. The same applies to anything you write to us directly on Instagram.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect payment details through this website.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, suggest properties that match your requirement and arrange site visits. We do not sell your information.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics tools and does not run advertising trackers. On the home page it stores a small flag in your browser session so the short opening animation plays only once per visit. It is removed when you close the tab.

Other services this website uses

  • Google Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google. Google receives your IP address and browser details when your browser requests the fonts.
  • Hosting. The site is delivered by a hosting provider, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and time of visit.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps. Buttons on this website open these services in a new tab. Their own privacy policies apply once you are there.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to handle your enquiry and to keep ordinary business records. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, withdraw your consent and make a complaint about how we handle it. To do any of these, write to us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. If we cannot resolve your complaint, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This website is meant for adults who are buying, selling or enquiring about property. It is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date above.
